# Spyglass

Spyglass is an activity tracker and companion for Old School RuneScape. It keeps your sessions, loot and progress together, whether you're working through Slayer tasks, training skills, killing bosses or playing minigames. Your history lets you look back at what you did, what you earned and how the session went.

It also helps with the regular stops along the way. Prepare your farming and bird house runs, check the supplies you need and follow guidance between patches. Keep an eye on your kingdom, see which dailies and weeklies are available, and pick up where you left off.

The goal is for Spyglass to become a companion you can use throughout your account's progression. We're building toward more skills and activities, giving each one useful information and a clean interface. Whether you have twenty minutes for a quick run or an evening to work toward a goal, Spyglass should help you keep track of it.

## Getting started

Install Spyglass from the RuneLite Plugin Hub and open it from the sidebar. It has four tabs: **Home**, **Session**, **Loot** and **History**.

Sessions, loot and history need no setup. Play as you normally would and Spyglass recognises what you're doing. Farming and bird house runs are something you start yourself from Home, when you want a plan and guidance.

## Session

The Session tab shows what you're doing right now.

- **Automatic sessions** for skilling, combat, Slayer, bosses and supported minigames, picked back up when you return from the bank or a short break.
- **XP** per skill, with level progress and XP per hour. Rates appear after five minutes of activity, so one large drop can't give an unrealistic hourly rate.
- **Loot** for the session, valued at Grand Exchange or High Alchemy prices.

### Smart Slayer

- Your current task with kills remaining, progress and the Slayer XP left.
- Kills, kills per hour and loot per kill, counted from the kills you actually make, including extra kills such as Death spawns and superiors.
- Reward points, task streak and when your next bonus task is.
- Your Slayer master, Konar's task location and Mortimer's task modifiers when the game shows them, plus Bracelet of slaughter and Expeditious bracelet effects.
- **Task History** for the session: each task's kills, loot and XP, and any superiors you killed. XP can be shown for every task or only the current one.
- Bosses killed on a matching task, such as Kraken on a Cave kraken task, stay part of your Slayer session.

### Skill Companions

Mining, Fishing, Agility, Woodcutting, Runecraft, Herblore and Smithing each have a companion that appears when you train the skill. It shows what you've made, what you've banked and how your current trip is going, with details that suit the skill:

- **Mining:** ore and gems mined, banked, and your gem bag.
- **Fishing:** catches, bait and supplies, and your fish barrel.
- **Agility:** laps, best lap, Marks of Grace and falls on each course.
- **Woodcutting:** logs cut, the tree you're on, Forestry events and your log basket.
- **Runecraft:** runes crafted, trips and runes per trip, and essence pouches.
- **Herblore:** potions and doses made by recipe, ingredients used, Potion Storage, and what your bank can still make.
- **Smithing:** bars, anvil work and cannonballs, Blast Furnace bars and coal, and your coal bag.

Set a **target** for any of these skills, either a level or a stockpile of what you're making. Spyglass shows what's left and an estimate once it has enough of your session to go on.

### Minigames and activities

- **Guardians of the Rift:** the current game, games per hour, reward points, average and best points, and an exact count of runes crafted. Rewards Guardian pulls are tracked as loot.
- **Tempoross:** energy, essence and storm intensity, games, permits and points.
- **Wintertodt:** your round, the time between rounds and the Reward Cart, with the rewards you've earned this session.
- **Pest Control:** games won and Commendation points earned.
- **Barrows:** the brothers you've killed this run and chests opened.
- **Giants' Foundry:** tracked as one session across many games.

## Loot

- **Grouped:** totals per source (NPC, boss or activity reward), with the kill count where RuneLite reports one.
- **Individual:** your drops in the order they happened, newest first, each labelled with its source.
- Search across every source, mark favourites, hide items you don't care about, and switch between Grand Exchange and High Alchemy values.
- Brimstone Chest rewards are listed as their own source, separate from your Slayer task.
- Right-click a source to reset its displayed totals. Your recorded history is kept.
- Loot from the past year is rebuilt from your saved data each time you log in.

## History

- Your sessions from the last 10 days, newest first. Short sessions are grouped under **Show short records** so the list stays easy to read.
- Open a session to see its duration, summary, XP, loot, what you made, and the equipment and inventory you started with.
- Slayer sessions list each task. Skill Companions and minigames show their own summaries.

## Home

Home brings together the things you check in on regularly. Each card shows its most important status at a glance; click it to open the full page.

### Farming

- **Overview:** every patch type you use, showing what's ready, growing or dead. Patches you haven't visited yet are shown as not tracked rather than guessed.
- **Runs:** pick a patch type, choose which patches to include and press **Start Run**. To do several types in one trip, turn on **Combine run types**, click the categories you want and press **Start combined run**.
- **Preparation:** before you set off, Spyglass lists the seeds, compost, tools, payments and teleports the run needs, checks what you're carrying and highlights what's still in your bank.
- **Guidance:** during the run Spyglass shows your next stop, marks the path, highlights the patch and the teleport to use, and ticks patches off as you finish them.
- **Travel:** choose the **fastest journey**, **Prefer my house** to travel through your house's portals and nexus, or **Use current setup** to stick to your current spellbook and the teleports you already have.
- **Compost:** choose the compost for each patch type, and see your compost bins with their contents. Bins can join a run on their own or alongside your crops.
- Your Farming contract and tree-clearing choices are covered too.

### Bird houses

See when your bird houses on Fossil Island will be full, then start a run. Spyglass lists the logs, clockworks and seeds you need, guides you between the spots (including the magic mushtrees), and keeps track as you empty and rebuild each one. An optional notification tells you when they're full.

### Kingdom

Check your Kingdom of Miscellania's approval and coffer, and whether the coffer can still pay the full daily amount. The page tells you what to do next. On the islands, a small overlay shows your approval and coffer as you work.

### Dailies & Weeklies

Tick the activities you do, such as daily claims, impling jars and Tears of Guthix, and see which are ready. Ready activities show as icons on the Home card; click one to jump straight to it. An optional notification tells you when Tears of Guthix is available.

### Coming soon

**PvP** is planned and shows as Coming soon on Home.

## Text size and contrast

Choose **Standard**, **Large** or **Extra large** text and **Standard** or **High** contrast for every Spyglass page. Change it from **Home → Text size & contrast**, with a live preview, or in the plugin settings.

## Settings

- **Text size & contrast:** as above.
- **Loot valuation:** value and sort loot by Grand Exchange or High Alchemy price.
- **XP / Loot order:** which section the Session tab shows first.
- **Slayer XP scope:** count XP for every task this session or only your current task. You can also switch this from the XP section.
- **Reminders:** notifications for full bird houses and Tears of Guthix.
- **Kingdom overlay:** show or hide the overlay on Miscellania and Etceteria.

Farming options such as travel, compost and tree clearing are on the Farming page.

## Your data

Spyglass stores your sessions, loot and progress on your own computer, per account:

```
.runelite/plugin-data/spyglass/<account>/
```
